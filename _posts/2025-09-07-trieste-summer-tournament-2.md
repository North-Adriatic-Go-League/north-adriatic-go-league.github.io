On **September 7, 2025**, Trieste hosted the second edition of the **Trieste Summer Tournament**. Players from **Italy and Slovenia** competed in three rounds.

**Top 3:**
1. Tianran Li (4k, Udine)
2. Uros Mihelcic (5k, Sežana)
3. Marko Potrata (7k, Sežana)
