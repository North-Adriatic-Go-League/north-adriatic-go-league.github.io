This year, together with the now tradicional [Artur Jonak von Freyenwald tournament](/events/), which will be held on Saturday October 17th, we'll also organize a full day workshop with Cho Seok-Bin:

## Nakamura Honinbo Go camp

Hello, I’m Cho Seok-Bin. I taught Go in Europe from 2005 to 2008. In 2013, I took over a small
Go school called “Nakamura Honinbo” in Nagoya, Japan, and since then six of my students
have become professional Go players in Japan.

This year, I will be attending the Poreč tournament as an instructor. After the tournament,
I would like to invite you to my Go workshop in Poreč, Croatia!

During the workshop, you will have lectures, teaching games, and detailed game reviews, we
will help you improve both your playing skills and your competitive mindset.
We hope to see you there!

Date: Date October 18th (Sun) 2026 10:00

## About Cho Seok-bin, 8Dan

* 49 wins in European Go tournaments, European Go rating: 2799
* Amateur Kisei in Japan 2014-2016
* Running a Go school in Japan (Nakamura Honinbo)
* Six students became professional in Japan

## Contents

* League game (you will get a review after the game)
* Teaching game (you will have at least 1 teaching game)
* Lectures (endgame and opening)
