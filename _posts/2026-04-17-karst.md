The traditional Go tournament in the heart of the Teran region 2026 is now behind us. This time, we hosted 21 players from Slovenia, Italy, and Croatia at the Širca Kodrič farm, where they treated us to their culinary delights.

The tournament was also honored by Mr. Andrej Sila, Mayor of the Municipality of Sežana, who watched the Go game with interest and promised future support.

This year, the tournament was also dedicated to Kosovel, with the best players receiving personalized bowls featuring Srečko Kosovel motifs as prizes.

The top three players were:

* 1st place – **Maja Logar**,
* 2nd place – **Andrew Swan**,
* 3rd place – **Andrej Kralj**,

...all three from the Go Society of Ljubljana.

At the end of the tournament, we also visited the wine cellar, which Ms. Martina kindly showed us.

Official results [here](https://europeangodatabase.eu/EGD/Tournament_Card.php?&key=T260517B).
