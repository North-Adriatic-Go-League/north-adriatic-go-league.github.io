Another successful tournament "GO in the Heart of the Teran Land 2025" is behind us.
At the Širca Kodrič farm, 22 players from Slovenia and Italy competed in five rounds.
💪The best were:
1. Janez Janža, GO Society Maribor
2. Andrew Swan, GO Society Ljubljana
3. Dušan Milavec, GO Society Sežana

[All results](https://europeangodatabase.eu/EGD/Tournament_Card.php?&key=T250518A&fbclid=IwY2xjawQCOmxleHRuA2FlbQIxMABzcnRjBmFwcF9pZBAyMjIwMzkxNzg4MjAwODkyAAEe8kT8_ELTi08E2M1f4EDTLXglRO7S7CQNPmSeh8GmaKRI8iXof8heSiedjWs_aem_zZosz2nu4vi02dEa1zrrcw)

Players and companions were very pleased with the hospitality at the farm - homemade cured meats, jota with sausage, homemade bread and of course an excellent drop of wine, as befits the month of celebrating Teran.
A lovely surprise was prepared by the Italians for their fellow player - Davide Bertok celebrated his three hundredth tournament participation this time. The cake was sweet. 😋
