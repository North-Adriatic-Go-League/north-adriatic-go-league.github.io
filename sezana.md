The Sežana GO association is an association that takes care of the development, mass and progress of the GO mental game, especially in the area of the Karst. 

The association continues the tradition of coastal GO clubs, especially GO club Pivka, as 4 founding members of the association were members of GO club Pivka in the past.

GO društvo Sežana was founded in 2014 with the aim of connecting players from Primorska. We want to bring the mental game GO to elementary school youth in Sežana and the Karst. 

The members of the association are from Pivka, Montinjan, Cerknica and Sežana.
