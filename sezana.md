Sežana GO club (orig. GO društvo Sežana) was founded in 2014, has 10 members within a 40 km radius of Sežana. We meet occasionally, participate in most competitions in Slovenia, Friuli Venezia Giulia and Croatian Istria.

We cooperate with two primary schools nearby and carry out the GO hobby activity.
