In November, the North Adriatic Go with Tesuji Go Club community gathers in the beautiful coastal city of **Poreč** for the **Memorial Jonakov**. This tournament stands as a tribute to the legacy of the game in our region, combining competitive play with a deep sense of tradition.

The event will bring together players from across Croatia, Italy, and Slovenia to honor the memory of one of our community's influential figures through the silent conversation of Baduk.

### Tournament Overview
The Memorial is designed to be a high-level meeting point for the autumn season. While specific details are currently being organized, the event will follow international tournament standards.

* **Location:** Poreč, Croatia
* **Atmosphere:** Commemorative & Competitive
