On March 22, 2026, the Societá Scacchistica Triestina 1904 in Trieste welcomed Go enthusiasts for a day of go with the "8th Trieste tournament" organized by the Higashikita Go Club.

The top 3 places were:

1. Gregor Butala (4d)
2. Andrej Kralj (3d)
3. Oliver Skocic (3d)

The complete results are here: [EGD Tournament Card](https://europeangodatabase.eu/EGD/Tournament_Card.php?&key=T260322A)
