We have been active in Poreč since 2023. That year, we organized our first go course and operated for several years as a section of the Capybara Association ([link](https://capybara.hr/)).

In 2025, we founded the go club **"Tesuji"**. The club is a member of the Croatian Go Federation and an associate member of the Poreč City Sports Community. We regularly organize the **"Artur Jonak von Freyenwald"** tournament in memory of the pioneer of the game of go in Europe (who was active in Pula at the beginning of the 20th century).

We meet every **Tuesday from 18:00 to 20:00** (Trg Antona Grabara, Poreč-Parenzo).
