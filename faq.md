## What is NAGL

The **North Adriatic Go League** (NAGL) is a cross-border community of Go clubs spanning **Italy, Slovenia, and Croatia**. We organize local tournaments, workshops, and regular meetups across the region.

## Where is NAGL?

Our members/clubs/events are located in:
- **Italy:** Trieste, Gorizia, Ronchi dei Legionari
- **Slovenia:** Sežana
- **Croatia:** Poreč

## How to join NAGL

There is no formal membership. Just show up at one of our club meetings or events — check the clubs and events pages for details.

The most important thing is to become friends, the rest will follow naturally, and one day you'll feel like you're also part of NAGL :)

## I don't know how to play go

No problem — beginners are welcome and we are happy to teach you.

## What's the relationship between NAGL and the national go federations?

NAGL is an informal group that makes it easier to organize cross-border events. Each club belongs to its own national federation:
- Italy: [FIGG](https://figg.org)
- Croatia: [HGS](https://hgos.hr/)
- Slovenia: [SGF](https://go-zveza.si/)
